## New features

- Animated temperature orb: orange/gold for heat, sky blue for cold, with the value below. Visibility toggle and customizable placement.
- Option to enable or disable Party Sync.
- Flashback replay compatibility.

## Improvements and fixes

- Improved synchronization of Chronotons, levels, required XP and maximum Dahal.
- Clearer Master level and upgrade cost display.
- Spell menu: refreshed XP, highlighted selected level and visible locked levels.
- Repair menu: main-hand item repair and removal of the extra dialog.
